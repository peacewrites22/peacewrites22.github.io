Writing 01

THE TRUTH

The truth For the first time in decades… The truth… With thoughts as wild as a man on aphrodisiac…this Author confess…DARKNESS INTRIGUES HER. Lights turned off Not for photophobia For the love of darkness feels Eyes closed I am home Envisioning darkness “I am scared for you” Her subconscious thought… Ohh hell! She’s scared for herself How warmed up she is in blankness As a child, she was punished for almost everything Being too sluggish…too thin… They’d lock her up in the dark So she wouldn’t be seen She never steps into the dark unless coerced A stray…in the dark. Slowly, she embraced darkness Then she realized she hadn’t been honest with herself the whole time She is darkness!

Writing 02

RED. YELLOW. GREEN.

Red means stop. Yellow means wait. Green means go. At least, that is what they taught us. But nobody taught us that sometimes green can still feel terrifying. Sometimes the door opens and we are still standing there. Sometimes "go" doesn't mean you're ready. Maybe courage isn't the absence of fear. Maybe courage is taking one small step while your hands are still shaking.

Writing 03

UNTITLED

Some days I don't want to disappear. I just want everything around me to become quiet. No questions. No expectations. No pretending. Just silence. A place where I can exist without having to explain why existing feels so heavy sometimes. Maybe tomorrow will be softer. For now, I am still here.
