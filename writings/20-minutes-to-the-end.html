20 MINUTES TO THE END

TWENTY MINUTES LEFT. Twenty minutes to become everything I kept postponing. Twenty minutes to say the things I swallowed whole because silence was easier than being understood. I wonder if the world would notice the absence of my footsteps, or if tomorrow would arrive exactly as planned— sunlight through windows, people rushing to places, someone laughing at something I will never hear. NINETEEN MINUTES… And suddenly, all the little things feel enormous. The unfinished sentence. The unopened message. The dress I never wore. The dream I kept saying I had time for. I spent so much of my life waiting for the right moment that I never noticed how quickly moments become memories. EIGHTEEN MINUTES If I could touch yesterday, I would. I'd give anything not to change everything. Just to hold myself a little longer. SEVENTEEN MINUTES I think about all the versions of me I abandoned along the way. The loud one. The hopeful one. The one who believed her life would become something beautiful. I wonder if she would recognize me now. Or if she would ask, quietly, "What happened?" SIXTEEN MINUTES I don't have an answer. Maybe nothing happened. Maybe everything did. Maybe growing up is just learning how to carry pieces of yourself without letting anyone see that they are missing. FIFTEEN 🕰️ Halfway to the end. And somehow, I am still thinking about tomorrow. Maybe that's the cruelest thing about being human. Even when the end is close, some stubborn little part of us still believes there will be a morning. FOURTEEN MINUTES 🕰️ THIRTEEN 🕰️ TWELVE 🕰️ Closed my eyes and 3 minutes gone… I count them like prayers. ELEVEN 🕰️ TEN 🕰️ I realize now I wasn't afraid of dying. I was afraid I had not lived enough. NINE MINUTES more… I wanted more. More mornings. More bad decisions. More apologies. More songs played too loudly. More big clothings, cause that's how I like them. More photographs where I actually liked myself. More chances to become the person I kept promising I would be. EIGHT SEVEN My life is a mess. A dilemma… If time is listening, let it slow down. Just this once. SIX T…I…k. To…k I have spent years trying to outrun the end without realizing the end was never chasing me. It was simply walking beside me. FIVE MINUTES FOUR There is so much I would say now. But words, like people, always seem to become more precious when we know we are about to lose them. THREE MINUTES TWO ONE And suddenly, I understand… The end was never the last minute. It was every minute I failed to notice while I was busy waiting for my life to begin. Twenty minutes ago, I thought I was dying. Now I know I was only finally learning how to live…
